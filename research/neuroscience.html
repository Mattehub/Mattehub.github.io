Neuroscience

I have been investigatin the hierarchical structure of interactions levaraged by the brain to integrate information and sustain cognitive functions. In a collaborative project, we benchmarked many recently developed methods based on information theory and topological data analysis. I have been employing similar methods to investigating causal learning analysing MEG and sEEG data to understand how neural activity encodes information about causal relationships in the environment.
