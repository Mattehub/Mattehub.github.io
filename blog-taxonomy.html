A taxonomy of neuroscientific strategies based on interaction orders

European Journal of Neuroscience · 2025 · Read the EJN paper

Neuroscientific strategies can be organized by the interaction order they study: pairwise, triplet, and higher-order group effects. The definition of the spectrum of interactions orders provides a new dimension that needs to be explored to build a comprehensive understanding of brain function.

Key idea: to understand brain function, we need to investigate different orders of Interactions as well as to investigate different spatio temporal scales or employ different techniques.

We review how different experimental and computational strategies map onto interaction orders. This taxonomy helps clarify how different methods complement each other and the level of explanation they can provide.

The framework is designed as a practical guide, not just a classification, and it aims to make higher-order concepts accessible to the broader neuroscience community.
