Neuronal avalanches in naturalistic speech and music listening

Preprint on bioRxiv · 2023 · Read the preprint

Neuronal avalanches are cascades of activity that reflect how the brain balances stability and flexibility. We analyze avalanches during naturalistic listening to speech and music.

Key idea: avalanche patterns track cognitive processing in real-life auditory conditions.

Using MEG recordings, we characterize the size and duration of avalanche events and relate them to stimulus structure. The analysis reveals consistent avalanche signatures that differ between speech and music, suggesting domain-specific processing strategies.

The results support the view that avalanche dynamics are a useful marker for cognitive engagement in ecologically valid settings.
