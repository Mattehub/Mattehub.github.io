About

Hello! I am a mathematician and complex systems physicist, currently completing a PhD in computational neuroscience. My research focuses on how intelligent behavior emerges in both natural and artificial systems, such as the human brain, neural networks, and society. Specifically, my research aims at (i) quantifying emergent behaviors in complex systems, (ii) uncovering the mechanisms that lead to such emergent behaviors, and (iii) developing methods to intervene and optimize or repair these mechanisms. By investigating these questions, I strive to improve our ability to predict, understand, and control complex systems, to address critical challenges of our century, particularly in the field of clinical neuroscience and AI safety.
